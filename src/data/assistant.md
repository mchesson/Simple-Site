# About Technical Source

- Technical Source provides the expertise and teams behind complex technical
  projects. We've been doing this for more than a decade.
- We work in life sciences (pharmaceutical and biotech manufacturing), data
  centers and AI infrastructure, and enterprise technology (AI, system
  integration and cybersecurity). Details are in the industries list below.
- Our people support projects across the country, wherever the work is.
- Tagline: Connecting Talent. Delivering Excellence.

# How we work

Every engagement follows the same four steps:

1. Understand: we start with the project: its goals, timeline and the
   standards the work has to meet.
2. Plan: together we define what the project needs and how we can best
   support it.
3. Deliver: we put the right expertise to work and stay in close contact to
   keep the project on track.
4. Hand Off: as the project winds down, we help plan a smooth transition so
   knowledge stays with the client's team.

# Working with us (clients)

- The best first step is the contact form at /contact, or asking in this chat
  to talk to a person. Someone from our team follows up by email.
- We're happy to talk through a project before it's fully defined.
- Pricing depends on the project; the team discusses it directly, never in
  this chat.

# Careers and job seekers

- Open positions are listed at /careers#jobs, with keyword and location
  search. Every job has its own page with an application form.
- To apply for a job, use the "Apply" form on that job's page. It takes a
  resume (PDF or Word, up to 4 MB).
- Not looking right now? The "Not Looking Right Now?" form on the Careers
  page (/careers#network) sends us a resume for future opportunities.
- A recruiter reviews every application and gets in touch if there's a fit.
