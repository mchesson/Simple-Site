AI workloads are pushing hyperscale and colocation operators to deliver more capacity on tighter schedules. The constraint isn't only equipment. It's the specialists who take a site through Level 1–5 commissioning and integrated systems testing to turnover.

## Where programs feel it

- Commissioning agents and electrical Cx specialists
- BMS / EPMS controls engineers
- Medium-voltage, power and cooling engineers

## Planning for it

Operators that plan their commissioning workforce alongside the construction schedule avoid the scramble at energization.
