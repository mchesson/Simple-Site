Technical Source is entering its eleventh year with a sharper focus: being the execution partner for organizations delivering complex, project-based programs in technically demanding environments.

## Three program types

That focus covers three kinds of work that share the same demands:

- **Life sciences manufacturing:** greenfield builds, brownfield retrofits, and commissioning, qualification and validation programs.
- **Data centers and AI infrastructure:** hyperscale and colocation build-outs, mission-critical MEP and commissioning.
- **Enterprise technology:** AI integration and enterprise security programs with defined scope and real consequences.

## What ties them together

Each is program-based, complex, technically demanding and high-consequence. In each, specialized talent is the constraint, and a missed window costs real money.

## Where we're headed

Today we deliver through staffing. Over the next few years we're growing into managed staffing and deliverable-based work, taking on more accountability for outcomes alongside our clients.
