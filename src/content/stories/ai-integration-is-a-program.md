Enterprise AI platforms are easy to buy and hard to put to work. The value comes from integration: connecting models to data, workflows and security controls on a defined timeline.

## Staff it like a program

- AI systems and MLOps engineers who have deployed to production
- Data platform engineers who can make the data usable
- Security architects involved from day one, not at go-live
